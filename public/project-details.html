Project Overview

The Mariners Group Expansion project aims to revolutionize the local shipping and logistics infrastructure in Chittagong. By becoming a co-owner, you are investing in a verified, highly profitable, and interest-free business model that complies strictly with Shariah principles.

Funds raised will be allocated towards acquiring new logistics vessels, expanding current warehouse facilities, and integrating modern supply chain technology to maximize operational efficiency and profit margins.
